Dr. Faladrum Sharma

Project Officer At Centre for Indian Knowledge Systems

Dr. Faladrum Sharma is Project Officer at the Centre for Indian Knowledge Systems, Indian Institute of Technology Guwahati. His ResearchGate profile lists a B.Tech. in Mechanical Engineering from Gauhati University, an M.Tech. in Mechanical Engineering from NIT Silchar, and a Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering from IIT Guwahati.

As of April 3, 2026, ResearchGate lists 17 publications, 1,218 reads, and 73 citations on his profile. His work spans additive manufacturing, 3D printing, sustainable manufacturing, industrial engineering, and related mechanical systems research.

Springer Nature currently lists nine indexed publications under his researcher page, including recent work on laser surface alloying, cold metal transfer technology, and manufacturing-focused educational design. Use the source links below to explore his profile pages.

Experience

ResearchGate lists Dr. Faladrum Sharma's current position as Project Officer at the Centre for Indian Knowledge Systems, IIT Guwahati. The same profile also records earlier IIT Guwahati roles including Post Doctoral Intern at CIKS, Research Associate in the Department of Mechanical Engineering, and Junior Research Fellow in Mechanical Engineering.

Across these positions, his work has focused on additive manufacturing, process modeling, materials processing, and the translation of research into CIKS-led academic and outreach initiatives.

His academic timeline on ResearchGate records B.Tech. studies at Gauhati University from 2010 to 2014, M.Tech. studies at NIT Silchar from 2014 to 2016, and Ph.D. work at IIT Guwahati from 2017 to 2022.

Springer Nature complements this profile with publication records on cold metal transfer technology, additive manufacturing assessment, selective laser sintering, fused deposition modelling, and laser surface alloying.
